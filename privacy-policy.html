Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · NEWON Technologies

This Privacy Policy explains how NEWON Technologies (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Life Legacy mobile application and related services (the “Service”).

1. Who we are

Life Legacy is operated by NEWON Technologies. For privacy questions, contact us at privacy@newontechnologies.com.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect:

  • Account information such as email address, name, password (stored in hashed form by our auth provider), and profile details you choose to provide.
  • Vault content you add, including assets, documents, notes, important contacts, emergency contact details, and Vault Trustee information and access settings.
  • Device contacts only if you choose to import contacts from your phone.
  • Security and device data such as vault PIN/biometric unlock preferences stored on your device, push notification tokens, app version, and basic diagnostic/crash information.
  • Usage data needed to operate features such as check-in reminders, notifications, and update checks.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage your account and vault;
  • Store and display your vault content securely;
  • Invite and manage Vault Trustees according to the permissions you set;
  • Operate Quiet period / Pause checks and related release controls;
  • Send service emails and push notifications you enable;
  • Provide support, improve reliability, and keep the Service secure;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information:

  • With Vault Trustees you invite, only according to the access permissions and release conditions you configure;
  • With service providers that help us run the Service (for example cloud hosting, authentication, storage, email, and push notification providers such as Supabase and Firebase);
  • If required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

5. Storage, security, and retention

Vault content is stored using cloud infrastructure with access controls. Sensitive unlock methods such as your vault PIN are handled on-device where applicable. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information.

We retain information while your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, resolve disputes, and meet legal requirements. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

6. Your choices

  • Update profile and vault content in the app;
  • Control Vault Trustee invitations and permissions;
  • Manage notification preferences where available;
  • Request access, correction, or deletion by emailing us.

7. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. International processing

Your information may be processed in countries where we or our providers operate. By using the Service, you understand that your information may be transferred to those locations.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date.
