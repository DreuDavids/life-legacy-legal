Request account & data deletion

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Life Legacy uses a 30-day delayed deletion process. When you request deletion, your account stays available during the waiting period so you can cancel if you change your mind.

Delete from the app (recommended)

  1. Open Life Legacy and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with your password.

Your account will be scheduled for deletion in 30 days. Before that date, open Settings again and tap Cancel deletion to keep your account.

Request by email

Prefer email? Contact us from the same address registered to your Life Legacy account:

david@newon-tech.com

Please include:

  • Subject line: Life Legacy account deletion request
  • The email address on your Life Legacy account
  • Your full name (if set on your profile)
  • Confirmation that you want your account and associated vault data deleted

What we delete

When deletion completes after the waiting period (or when we process a verified email request), we delete your Life Legacy account and associated vault data we hold for that account, including profile information, assets, documents, notes, contacts, and Vault Trustee relationships tied to your account, except where we must retain limited information for security, fraud prevention, legal, or accounting reasons.

Processing time

In-app requests are scheduled immediately for deletion in 30 days. Email requests are confirmed and processed within 30 days after verification.

Before you delete

  • Download or save any documents you still need.
  • Understand that Vault Trustees will lose access to your vault content once the account is deleted.
  • Deletion cannot be undone after it completes.
