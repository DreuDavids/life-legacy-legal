Request account & data deletion

Last updated: 11 November 2026

If you have a Life Legacy account, you can request deletion of your account and associated data using the steps below.

How to request deletion

Email us from the same email address registered to your Life Legacy account:

david@newon-tech.com

Please include:

  • Subject line: Life Legacy account deletion request
  • The email address on your Life Legacy account
  • Your full name (if set on your profile)
  • Confirmation that you want your account and associated vault data deleted

What we delete

When we process a verified request, we delete your Life Legacy account and associated vault data we hold for that account, including profile information, assets, documents, notes, contacts, and Vault Trustee relationships tied to your account, except where we must retain limited information for security, fraud prevention, legal, or accounting reasons.

Processing time

We aim to confirm and complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. You may receive a follow-up email if we need to verify that the request came from the account owner.

Before you delete

  • Download or save any documents you still need.
  • Understand that Vault Trustees will lose access to your vault content once the account is deleted.
  • This action cannot be undone after deletion is completed.
